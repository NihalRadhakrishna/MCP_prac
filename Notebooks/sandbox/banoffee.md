# Banoffee Pie

A classic, crowd-pleasing banoffee pie with a buttery biscuit crust, rich caramel/toffee filling, fresh bananas, and whipped cream.

## Ingredients
- 225g digestive biscuits or graham crackers, crushed
- 100–150g butter, melted
- 1 can caramel or dulce de leche (about 397g)
- 3–4 ripe bananas, sliced
- 300–400ml heavy/double cream, whipped
- Optional: grated chocolate or cocoa for topping

## Method
1. Mix crushed biscuits with melted butter, then press into a pie dish.
2. Chill the crust until firm.
3. Spread the caramel/dulce de leche over the base.
4. Top with sliced bananas.
5. Whip the cream and pile it on top.
6. Finish with grated chocolate if desired, then chill before serving.

## Notes
- Best made a few hours ahead so it sets well.
- Use very ripe bananas for the best flavor.
- Serve cold.
